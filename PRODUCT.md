# PRODUCT.md

## Register
brand — this is a personal portfolio + blog. The design IS the product; the site's job is to make a strong, original impression and route people to the writing and work.

## Product Purpose
A minimal "identity hub" for Nitesh Balla that doubles as resume + blog. One striking landing screen (name, a short identity line, links, recent posts) backed by a real blog. It should read as the home of a serious engineer who also writes, and feel premium and hand-made, not templated.

## Users
- Recruiters / hiring managers scanning for credibility in seconds.
- Fellow engineers who arrive from a blog post or GitHub and want to know who wrote it.
- Peers / curious readers who came for the writing (Loki multi-tenancy, JS closures, more to come).

## Brand & Tone
- Voice: precise, understated, a little dry. Confident without selling.
- Identity: Forward Deployed Engineer at Manif (payments). Payments-deep engineer who embeds with customers and ships across the stack to make systems work in the real world. Backend / infrastructure roots, frontend-capable. Writes about the hard, unglamorous parts of running software in production.
- Headline title: lead with "Forward Deployed Engineer" + payments depth. Do NOT use the generic "full stack" label; FDE already implies breadth and reads more senior.
- Feeling to land in the first second: "this person has taste and knows their craft." Premium, considered, original.

## Anti-references (do NOT do)
- nishantjosh.dev — the rotating globe / orbit generative background. We admire the restraint but must not copy the signature. No spheres, no orbital line bursts.
- Generic dark-mode dev portfolio with gradient blobs and a hero-metric grid.
- Templated SaaS landing aesthetic (gradient text, identical card grids, glassmorphism-by-default).

## Strategic Principles
- Say almost nothing, but make the one screen unforgettable. Depth (blog, work) lives one click / one scroll away.
- Originality over trend-chasing. Trends (grain, bento, mono) are tools, used only if they serve the impression.
- Two candidate directions to compare:
  1. Editorial-serif + film grain, warm/light, writer-coded.
  2. Dithering / 1-bit black-and-white print aesthetic, rare and developer-coded.
- Must stay maintainable on the existing Astro stack. Low-JS, ages well.
