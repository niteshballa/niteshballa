Hello! I'm Nitesh Balla, a passionate fintech developer who excels at building strong teams and embracing new technologies. Imperfections motivate me to refine and improve. Driven by a never-give-up mindset.

I'm a software engineer who enjoys working across the full stack, with particular expertise in:

- **Frontend Development**: JavaScript, TypeScript, ReactJs, NextJs
- **Cloud & DevOps**: AWS, Azure, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD
- **Database Technologies**: SQL, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis
- **Plugin Development**: Shopify, Shopware, WooCommerce, PlentyOne

- **Specialization**: Payment Systems, PCI and ISO Compliance

## My Professional Journey

After completing my studies in 2022, I embarked on my professional journey with Juspay as a Product Engineer, where I honed my skills in building scalable payment solutions. I was fortunate to contribute to Hyperswitch, an open source payment orchestration project initiated by Juspay, which deepened my expertise in fintech infrastructure.

Currently, I serve as a Tech Lead at GreenBanana Group Germany, working remotely to drive technology strategy and deliver impactful financial products.
