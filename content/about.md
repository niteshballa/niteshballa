I'm **Nitesh Balla**, a Forward Deployed Engineer at [Manif](https://manif.ai),
where we build the financial infrastructure and control plane for agentic
commerce — sitting close to customers, building against the specifics of their
problem, and shipping systems that survive contact with production.

## How I work

I'm drawn to the gap between what a product can do and what a customer actually
needs. That usually means writing code in the field, choosing which corners to
cut on purpose, and owning the result end to end. Most of what I've shipped
lives in payments, where the trade-offs are unforgiving and the failure modes
are public.

## What I work with

- **Backend** — Rust, C++, Java, Python, Node.js
- **Frontend** — TypeScript, React, Next.js
- **Cloud & infra** — AWS, Azure, Terraform, Docker, Kubernetes, CI/CD
- **Data** — PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis
- **Domain** — payment systems, PCI and ISO compliance

## Before this

I led engineering at **GreenBanana Group** (Germany), working remotely on
financial products. Before that, a product engineer at **Juspay**, building
payment systems and contributing to
[Hyperswitch](https://github.com/juspay/hyperswitch), the open-source payment
orchestrator.
